Automatic vs Manual Mileage Tracking: Which is Better?

Paper logs are free, but they might be costing you thousands in lost deductions.

For decades, the "glovebox notebook" was the gold standard for tracking mileage. You’d write down your odometer reading at the start and end of every trip. Simple, right? But as businesses move faster, manual tracking has become a liability.

The Hidden Cost of Manual Tracking

Manual tracking relies on memory. Studies show that drivers who manually log trips under-report their mileage by an average of 20-25%. Why? Because we ignore short trips.

A 3km drive to the post office? "I'll write it down later." (Narrator: He never wrote it down). Over a year, these forgotten trips can add up to huge tax savings lost.

Automatic Tracking: The Modern Standard

Automatic apps run in the background and use GPS to record every movement.

  • ✅ Accuracy: Captures exactly 100% of your drives.
  • ✅ Time: Saves ~3 hours of admin work per month.
  • ✅ Compliance: Creates audit-proof digital logs with maps.
